# Indian dashboard photography

Thirty photographs sourced from Wikimedia Commons: twenty distinct buyer and seller headers, five food catalogue photographs, one fruit collection photograph, and four jewellery editorial photographs. Source descriptions, Indian locations, photographer names, licenses and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in credits.json. A linked photo-credits.html page provides public attribution.

Responsive object-fit crops and resized thumbnails are the only photographic changes. Each image retains the individual source license; CC BY-SA images and their crops remain under the same license. No image is assigned to two different headers, editorial placements or catalogue items. The same item may show its photograph again in its detail dialog or basket.

The canonical EdgeMart logo is public/logo.svg: an original vector E/shop-bag mark in violet and orange, drawn for this project. The logo is brand artwork, rather than stock photography.
