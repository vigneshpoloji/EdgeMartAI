Privacy policy

This page explains what information EdgeMart AI uses to run accounts, shopping, deliveries and support.

Contact & operator

EdgeMart AI at edgemartai.onrender.com is operated by Poloji Vignesh. For privacy questions or requests, email vigneshpoloji@gmail.com.

Information we use

  • Accounts: name, email, account role and sign-in identity. Email/password accounts use a password hash for authentication.
  • Shopping & orders: selected items, order history, recipient name, email, phone, delivery address, instructions, delivery preferences and order status. Checkout currently supports cash on delivery; it does not collect payment-card details.
  • Seller & delivery work: store information, products, pickup location, driver phone, city, vehicle, availability and location updates shared for deliveries.
  • Locations: coordinates you enter or choose to share through browser location permission, to find nearby stores and support pickup, delivery and tracking.
  • Support & technical information: contact-form name, email, optional phone and message; chat messages; session and security information, such as IP addresses and request/error logs used to operate the service and limit abuse.

We use this information to sign you in, manage stores, fulfil and track orders, respond to support requests and protect the website.

Google & GitHub sign-in

If you choose Google or GitHub sign-in, we receive basic identity information needed to authenticate you, including your name, verified email and provider account identifier. We store account and provider identifiers to recognise your account and connected sign-in methods. Authentication credentials are processed by the backend during sign-in.

Google sign-in requests OpenID, basic profile and email access. GitHub sign-in requests profile and email access. These sign-in methods do not give EdgeMart access to the contents of your mailbox or Google Drive. We use Google account data for account access and related service features; we do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Who receives information

Sellers receive the order information needed to fulfil their own orders. An assigned delivery partner receives customer contact, delivery and location information needed to complete that delivery. Buyers can see the driver location updates provided for their order.

  • Render & MongoDB Atlas: hosting and database services process application and account/order data.
  • Brevo & Gmail: contact-form details and messages are sent through Brevo to the operator’s Gmail inbox. Your email is used as the reply address.
  • Google & GitHub: their own services process sign-in requests when you choose those methods.
  • OpenStreetMap: displayed maps and map links include coordinates and map areas; the map provider receives requests from your browser.
  • Google Gemini: processes chatbot text as described below.

These providers handle information under their own service terms and privacy practices. Hosting and provider processing may take place outside your country.

Ask EdgeMart

For an AI reply, we send your message, a limited recent chat history and public product catalogue information to Google Gemini. We do not automatically add your account email, password, private order details, delivery address or device location to the chatbot context. Anything you type into a message can still be sent to Google.

Use the chatbot for general product and service questions. Do not enter passwords, payment details, medical records or other private or confidential information.

Google’s processing depends on the Gemini service plan. Unpaid services may use messages and replies to improve Google products and may involve human review; paid services have different data-use terms. See the Gemini API terms. AI replies can be inaccurate; confirm purchases and order status through the website’s normal checkout and order pages.

Storage & security

Accounts, store information and order records are stored in MongoDB Atlas. Contact messages are retained in the operator’s mailbox and processed by the email provider. The chatbot does not currently store a persistent conversation history in EdgeMart’s database.

Necessary cookies maintain sign-in and protect requests. Browser storage also remembers shopping state and the delivery location you select. We use HTTPS on the public website, hashed passwords and account/role access checks. No internet service can guarantee complete security.

Account and order records remain while needed to operate the service and handle support, security and recordkeeping. There is currently no automatic account or order purge schedule. Provider logs and email copies are subject to the respective provider’s retention practices.

Your choices

You can decline browser location permission, enter coordinates manually, sign out, clear browser storage or revoke a connected provider’s access from its account settings. Clearing browser storage or revoking provider access does not by itself delete your EdgeMart account or orders.

Email the operator to request access, correction or removal of your information. We may need to verify that the account is yours and explain any records that must be retained for order handling, security or applicable obligations. For a support reply, contact us using the email associated with your account.

We update this page when our information practices change and show the revision date above.
