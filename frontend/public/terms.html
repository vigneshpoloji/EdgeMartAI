The service

EdgeMart AI is operated by Poloji Vignesh at edgemartai.onrender.com. It provides shopping, seller workspaces, delivery partner workspaces and order tracking. These terms describe how to use those features. Our privacy policy explains how information is handled.

Accounts

Provide accurate account and contact details, keep your password and sign-in access private, and use only accounts and information you are authorised to use. Email/password signup is followed by sign-in. Google and GitHub are optional sign-in methods subject to those providers’ requirements.

Do not impersonate someone, publish unlawful or misleading listings, misuse customer information, send abusive messages, bypass access controls or interfere with the service. Access may be restricted to investigate misuse or protect customers and the website.

Orders & payment

Check the item, quantity, seller, delivery details and total before confirming an order. Availability is checked again at confirmation and may change while browsing. The website shows prices and order totals in Indian rupees.

Payment currently uses cash on delivery. Pay the amount shown in the confirmed order when the delivery is completed. The website currently has no online card-payment checkout.

Product descriptions and images are supplied through seller listings. Contact support if an item is incorrect, damaged or otherwise has a problem. Return and refund requests are reviewed based on the order circumstances and applicable customer rights.

Delivery & cancellation

Provide an accurate delivery address, reachable phone number and saved delivery coordinates. The seller must have a pickup location, and the buyer’s delivery location must be available before a driver can accept pickup.

Orders move through confirmation, preparation, ready for pickup, out for delivery and delivered. Delivery dates or preferences and map updates are not guarantees of arrival time. Tracking shows the device updates received and can be delayed by connectivity or location permission.

You can cancel through My orders while the order is still confirmed, before preparation begins. If preparation has started, the dashboard does not offer self-service cancellation; contact support to discuss the order. Delivery location changes are restricted after pickup.

Sellers & delivery partners

Sellers must provide accurate store, pickup and product information, publish only products they are allowed to sell, keep stock and prices current, and update their own orders through the preparation sequence.

Delivery partners must provide accurate profile details, choose availability appropriately and update assigned deliveries truthfully. Share location only when appropriate for delivery work. Use customer contact and location details solely to fulfil the assigned delivery; do not reuse them for unrelated contact or advertising.

AI assistance & service availability

Ask EdgeMart provides general product and website guidance. AI replies may be inaccurate and do not confirm orders, change payments or guarantee stock or delivery times. Use the checkout and order dashboard for confirmed information. Do not rely on chatbot answers for medical decisions.

The website depends on hosting, database, email, sign-in and map providers. Service interruptions and delays can occur. The current free hosting may pause during inactivity, so the first visit after a pause can take longer.

Support & changes

For order, account, listing or delivery problems, use the website contact form or email vigneshpoloji@gmail.com. Include the order reference where relevant and never send a password or API key.

Nothing in these terms removes rights available under applicable consumer law. We may update service features and these terms; the revision date appears above. Contact the operator if you need clarification before using a feature.
